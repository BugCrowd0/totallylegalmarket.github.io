SUBMIT A SHOWCASE

Website accounts are no longer required. Showcase submissions are handled through the TLM Discord bot.

  1. Join the TLM Discord server.
  2. Run /upload showcase.
  3. Submit a Google Drive, TikTok, YouTube (public or unlisted), or Gofile link.
  4. Enter the username and title you want displayed.
  5. Wait for moderator approval before it appears on the site.

Make sure your link can be opened by people who have it. Google Drive links need sharing access enabled.
