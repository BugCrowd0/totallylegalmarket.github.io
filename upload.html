HOW TO SUBMIT A SHOWCASE

Share a video showing off your mod with the TLM community. You do not need a TLM account.

01 / JOIN THE DISCORD

Join the TLM Discord server using the link below.

JOIN DISCORD →

02 / SUBMIT YOUR SHOWCASE

In the server, run the /uploadshowcase slash command and follow the bot's prompts. Add a clear title and a link to your showcase video if requested.

03 / CHECK THE SHOWCASES PAGE

Once the bot has accepted your submission, visit the showcases page to see whether it has appeared.

VIEW SHOWCASES →
TLM does not require a website account for showcase submissions. Please use the Discord bot's prompts and only submit videos you have permission to share.
